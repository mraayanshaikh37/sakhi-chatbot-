# 🌸 Sakhi -- AI Safety Companion

> **An AI-powered safety and emotional support companion designed with
> accessibility, empathy, and emergency assistance in mind.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 📖 About

Sakhi is a browser-based AI companion created to provide a calm,
supportive, and accessible conversational experience. It combines
AI-powered conversations with practical safety features in a lightweight
mobile-friendly interface.

Originally conceived under the working title **Guardian Angel**, the
project evolved into **Sakhi**, focusing on simplicity, trust, and
support for everyday users.

# ⚙ Working Principle

1.  User enters a message.
2.  The message is sent to the AI API.
3.  Sakhi generates a contextual response.
4.  Conversation continues naturally within the session.
5.  During emergencies, shaking the phone three times opens the SOS
    interface.
6.  Sakhi retrieves the user's location and prepares a WhatsApp
    emergency message for a trusted contact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🚨 Emergency SOS

The Emergency SOS feature allows users to quickly prepare a WhatsApp
message containing their live location by shaking the phone three times
and selecting a trusted contact.
