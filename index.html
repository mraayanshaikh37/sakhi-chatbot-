📜 Right to Zero FIR

A Zero FIR can be registered at ANY police station in India, regardless of where the incident occurred. Police cannot refuse registration citing territorial jurisdiction.

🌙 No Arrest After Sunset

Under Section 46(4) CrPC, a woman cannot be arrested between sunset and sunrise except under exceptional circumstances with prior written permission from a Judicial Magistrate.

👮‍♀️ Female Police Officer Presence

Recording of statements or examination of a female victim/witness must be conducted by or in the presence of a woman police officer.

🩹 Emergency Medical First-Aid Guides

🩸 Severe Bleeding & Hemorrhage

  1. Apply firm, direct pressure on the wound using a clean cloth or bandage.
  2. Do NOT remove initial soaked cloth; add more layers on top.
  3. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if no fracture is suspected.
  4. If arterial spurting persists on limb, apply a tourniquet 2 inches above the wound.

🗣️ Choking (Heimlich Maneuver)

  1. Stand behind person, wrap arms around waist.
  2. Make a fist just above the navel; grasp with other hand.
  3. Deliver quick, upward and inward abdominal thrusts until airway clears.

🔥 Burns & Thermal Scalds

  1. Cool burn under gentle cool running tap water for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Do NOT use ice, butter, or toothpaste on burns.
  3. Cover loosely with sterile, non-stick cling wrap or dressing.

😵 Fainting & Heat Exhaustion

  1. Lay person flat on back; elevate legs 12 inches (30 cm).
  2. Loosen tight collars and clothing; fan with cool fresh air.
  3. Turn to side (Recovery Position) if vomiting or breathing irregularly.
🚨 Disaster & Crisis Action Hub
🚑 Call 108 Ambulance

🔥 Building Fire & Smoke Inhalation

  • Stay Low: Crawl beneath smoke where breathable oxygen is highest.
  • Feel Doors: Touch doorknobs with back of hand before opening; if hot, do not open.
  • If Clothes Catch Fire: STOP, DROP, and ROLL to extinguish flames.

🌍 Earthquake Protocol

  • DROP, COVER, HOLD ON: Drop to knees under a sturdy desk/table.
  • Stay away from glass windows, exterior walls, and falling light fixtures.
  • Do NOT use elevators during or immediately after tremors.

🏃‍♂️ Crowd Surge & Stampede Defense

  • Adopt a Boxer Stance with arms bent in front of chest to protect lungs/ribcage.
  • Never fight against the crowd flow; move diagonally towards the edges or perimeter.
  • If knocked down, curl into a tight fetal ball covering head and neck.
